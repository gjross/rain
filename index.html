How this works
  • The shaded range is the middle 80% of 82 forecast runs: the ECMWF (51) and GFS (31) ensembles. A wide band means the models genuinely don't know yet. It narrows as Saturday gets closer.
  • NBM is the National Blend of Models, NOAA's bias-corrected mix of many models. It's the closest thing to an official forecast, so it's the headline number.
  • HRRR is NOAA's 3 km storm-scale model. Its long runs reach 48 hours, so it joins Thursday afternoon and covers the whole day by Friday morning.
  • Air quality is the CAMS 5-day forecast (US AQI). It first reaches Saturday early in the week.
  • Pulls only happen when you tap Refresh. "Next useful refresh" is when a model run that could change the answer lands.
  • Finals and awards times are estimates from BOA's usual regional format. The prelims schedule is BOA's published one.
